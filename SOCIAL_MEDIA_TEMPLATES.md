## ❌ NEVER POST

- Customer numbers (unless verified)
- Revenue claims
- Specific performance metrics
- Competitor comparisons
- "World's first/only/best"
- Medical/financial use cases
- Guarantees of any kind
- False urgency ("Only 3 spots left!")
- Fake testimonials
- Unsubstantiated ROI claims

## ✅ ALWAYS INCLUDE

- Beta/Preview labels when applicable
- "Features subject to change" for beta content
- Links to Terms of Service
- Real metrics only
- Factual feature descriptions
- Appropriate disclaimers

---

Remember: Honest, transparent marketing builds trust and avoids legal issues. When in doubt, be conservative with claims.
